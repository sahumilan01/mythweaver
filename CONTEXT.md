# MythWeaver

MythWeaver is a shared visual storytelling space in which a person and a browser agent make a story world together without obscuring who authored each contribution.

## Language

**Story World**:
The complete creative artifact containing visual elements, their relationships, and the events that give them narrative meaning.
_Avoid_: Whiteboard, document, canvas file

**Story Element**:
A meaningful thing in the Story World, such as a character, place, object, or event.
_Avoid_: Node, shape, asset

**Story Beat**:
One ordered moment in the story, focused on one or more Story Elements.
_Avoid_: Slide, frame, timeline item

**Contribution**:
A set of related creative changes made by either the person or the agent.
_Avoid_: Generation, output, response

**Proposal**:
An uncommitted agent Contribution presented for human review.
_Avoid_: Draft, suggestion layer, generated result

**Creative Consent**:
The rule that an agent Proposal becomes part of the Story World only through an explicit human choice.
_Avoid_: Approval flow, confirmation step

**Provenance**:
The visible authorship of a Contribution: human, agent, or mixed.
_Avoid_: Ownership, audit log

**Performance**:
A guided experience of the Story World that presents its Story Beats in order.
_Avoid_: Slideshow, animation export, playback mode
